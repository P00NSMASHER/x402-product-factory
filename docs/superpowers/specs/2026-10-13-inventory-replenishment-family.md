# Inventory replenishment family — design

**Owner intent:** A truly autonomous factory that continuously creates useful apps without repeated prompting. Previous work supported (a) document intake and (b) estimate-to-order workflow. Deliver a third **distinct domain**, not another renamed checklist or quote calculator.

**Alternatives:** (1) Expand document or quote titles—rejected as superficial. (2) Connected real purchasing and automated payments—rejected because it would spend funds and violate commercial authorization. (3) Dedicated inventory readiness and internal replenishment engine—selected: a concrete buyer-facing work loop with measurable stock availability, forecast shortages, separate review and safe simulated fulfillment.

**Data model:** validated versioned recipe with 3–12 stock SKUs, per-SKU reorder point, target level and explicitly sample unit cost. Durable local SQLite has multiple tenants and 43-character opaque operator/reviewer keys hashed at rest. Per-item serializable snapshots with physical on-hand, reserved, on-order, revisions, history and internal proposal states; HMAC-style idempotency request semantics via saved command digests. All quantities integer; all illustrative costs integer cents; no negative stock or ambiguous partial quantities. Row/version boundary rejects stale writes. Background service can automatically propose, NEVER approve/purchase.

**User interface and safety:** All assets first-party; no external fonts/scripts, no browser storage, strict CSP, local-only Host/Origin-guarded HTTP. Mobile/desktop interface displays separate stock, reserved, availability, expected internal arrivals and nonbinding cost estimates; reviewer can approve/reject, operator can receive after internal approval. No real financial transaction, supplier message or client identity. Errors fail closed. All generated artifacts are content-addressed, source-authenticated-by-digest and independently tested from packaged bytes. Date limits and receipt quotas bounded.

**Commercial boundary:** sample suppliers/costs cannot establish real demand, margin, procurement eligibility or compliance. Commercial HOLD; no public deployment, emailed invitations, spending, payments, new paid product numbers, or merging. Native browser, process restart, idempotence, roles, tenant isolation and full existing release gates required before describing an app as technically tested.
