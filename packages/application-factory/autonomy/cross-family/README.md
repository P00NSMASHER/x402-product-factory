# Cross-family hypothesis to full-app manufacturing

Fourteen structurally differentiated buyer-workflow hypotheses (seven quote-to-internal-order, seven inventory replenishment) are entered as **problems, triggers and inputs**, not as full application source code. The factory automatically derives validated application recipes, assigns deterministic safe example defaults, compiles independently runnable role-separated applications and tests real SQLite+HTTP lifecycles.

The cited NIST manufacturing and federal acquisition reference pages establish **broad operational context only**. They do not establish market demand, current prices, verified customers, regulatory compliance or real commercial economics. All inventory unit costs are fictional examples; synthetic quote values are user-entered in local tests. Technical-fit scores measure structural readiness only.

    node scripts/manufacture-cross-family-ideas.js --check
    node scripts/manufacture-cross-family-ideas.js --build --quote-output /absolute/private/quotes --inventory-output /absolute/private/inventory

A new source proposal must pass exact schema, safe HTTPS, non-PII, source-date, unique item, novelty and separate-family-kernel validation. The source order remains stable; app identities must not be silently rewritten after reviewed packages exist. New items can be added in the bounded queue, but there is no claim of unrestricted AI generation.

CI independently builds all fourteen synthetic apps, then subjects them to the private deployment probe. Public hosting, actual payments, checkout, outreach, customer data and new Product 025+ remain blocked.

## Append-only dated research extension

The original eight source-entry objects are locked as an unchanged prefix by `original-proposals-20261013.json`, alongside historical accepted app SHA receipts. Six additional **unverified** EPA/DOE contextual workflows were added on October 14, 2026. The corpus `observedOn` now equals the most recent source observation. Each proposal independently checks its two source dates within a 180-day observation span; future corpus extensions **do not invalidate** older source-hashed entries simply because time passes. A future new proposal may append at the end with a newer observation date and advance the corpus watermark. Source URLs provide process context only, **not proof of buyers, willingness to pay, compliance certification, or actual transactions**. Original and appended entry SHA digests remain unique and independently reverified by the existing package acceptance gate. A prefix change or forged watermark is refused.
